About Me

I'm a Software Firmware Engineer with expertise in embedded systems development across x86 and ARM architectures. My background includes building secure boot chains for Yocto/Embedded Linux, optimizing Windows drivers in C++, and integrating IoT solutions with AWS IoT Core and MQTT. I've worked extensively with protocols including UART, SPI, I2C, BLE, and CAN, and platforms like ESP32, STM32, NVIDIA Jetson, and Qualcomm Cortex-A series.

At ADLINK Technology, I have delivered customer-focused firmware solutions, including platform bring-up, custom Linux implementations with Secure Boot, and low-level platform software for x86 and ARM systems. At Axonne Inc., I led NVIDIA Jetson-based video pipeline development and created FTDI-based debugging tools with custom C++ GUIs. I'm passionate about hardware-software integration, real-time systems, and building reliable embedded products from bootloader to cloud.
